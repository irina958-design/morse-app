Bundled performance preset data

The preset values in electron/main/services/performanceConfigData.ts are derived
from OptimizationLock by Sqooky and contributors and OptiLock by dacooder and
contributors. They retain GNU GPL version 3 terms. Morse's generated differences
are modifications of those configuration data, not a relicensing under MIT.

The original upstream license texts are optimizationlock.txt and optilock.txt
in this directory. Source pins, paths and hashes for every bundled version are
in scripts/performance-presets.json. The generator and modified data are in the
public Morse repository:
https://github.com/irina958-design/morse

OptimizationLock source:
https://github.com/Sqooky/OptimizationLock
OptiLock source:
https://github.com/dacooderr/OptiLock

These presets are configuration data. Their GPL notices do not remove the MIT
notices covering the original desktop code or Morse's other source.
